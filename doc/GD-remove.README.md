To remove access for any third-party app—whether it is a verified app or not—you must revoke its permissions directly from your Google Account settings.

1. **Open Google Security Settings:** Web Browser.
Navigate to [myaccount.google.com/security](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fmyaccount.google.com%2Fsecurity) and sign in with the Google account that is connected to the app.
*Verification:* The page loads with "Security" highlighted in the top or side navigation menu.


2. **Locate Connected Apps:** Scroll Down.
Scroll down to the section titled **Your connections to third-party apps and services** and click on **See all connections**.
*Verification:* A page displaying a list of all third-party apps and services linked to your account appears.


3. **Delete Connections:** Revoke Access.
Click on the specific app you want to remove. At the bottom of its details page, click **Delete all connections you have with [App Name]** (or **Remove access**), and click **Confirm** in the pop-up window.
*Verification:* The app is immediately removed from your list of connected apps, severing its access.
